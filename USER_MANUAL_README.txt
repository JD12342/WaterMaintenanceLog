WATER MAINTENANCE LOG SYSTEM - USER MANUAL

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The Water Maintenance Log System is a comprehensive digital platform designed to manage water infrastructure maintenance requests, complaints, and work orders. The system streamlines the process from initial complaint submission through final work completion and reporting.

The system serves four main user types: Consumers who report issues, Administrators who manage the workflow, Engineering staff who assess technical requirements, and Maintenance personnel who perform the actual repair work.

USER ROLES AND PERMISSIONS

CONSUMER ROLE
Consumers are residents or businesses who can submit complaints about water-related issues. Consumer users have the following capabilities:
- Submit new complaints about water system problems
- View their own submitted complaints and status updates
- Track progress of their complaints through the system
- Receive notifications about complaint status changes

ADMIN ROLE
Admin users manage the overall workflow and have supervisory access to all system functions:
- View and manage all complaints in the system
- Review and update complaint status
- Forward complaints to engineering for technical assessment
- Create work orders for approved complaints
- Assign work orders to maintenance staff
- Verify completion of maintenance work
- Generate reports and analytics
- Manage user accounts and permissions

ENGINEERING ROLE
Engineering staff provide technical assessment and approval for maintenance requests:
- Review complaints forwarded from admin
- Conduct technical assessments of reported issues
- Approve or decline complaints based on technical merit
- Provide damage assessments and repair recommendations
- Estimate required materials and labor hours
- Add technical notes and engineering specifications

MAINTENANCE ROLE
Maintenance personnel perform the actual repair work and report completion:
- View work orders assigned to them
- Update work order status as work progresses
- Submit detailed completion reports
- Record materials used and hours worked
- Upload photos of completed work
- Note any follow-up requirements

SYSTEM FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALITY

COMPLAINT SUBMISSION PROCESS
To submit a new complaint, consumers log into the system and provide the following information:
- Complaint title providing a brief description of the issue
- Detailed description of the problem including when it was first noticed
- Specific location where the issue is occurring
- Priority level if the consumer believes it requires urgent attention

The system assigns each complaint a unique identifier and tracks it through all subsequent stages of processing.

COMPLAINT REVIEW AND STATUS TRACKING
Admin users regularly review new complaints and update their status as they progress through the system. The complaint status options include:
- Pending: Newly submitted complaint awaiting initial review
- Reviewed: Admin has reviewed the complaint and determined next steps
- Submitted to Engineering: Complaint forwarded to engineering for technical assessment
- Approved: Engineering has approved the complaint for maintenance work
- Declined: Engineering has determined the complaint does not require maintenance intervention
- Assigned: Work order created and assigned to maintenance staff
- In Progress: Maintenance work is currently underway
- Completed: Maintenance work has been finished
- Closed: Final status indicating complaint resolution and closure

ENGINEERING ASSESSMENT PROCESS
When engineering staff review complaints, they create detailed assessment records that include:
- Decision to approve or decline the complaint
- Reason for the approval or decline decision
- Technical engineering assessment of the issue
- Recommended materials needed for repair work
- Estimated hours required to complete the work
- Any special instructions or safety considerations

WORK ORDER MANAGEMENT
For approved complaints, admin users create work orders that contain:
- Unique work order number for tracking purposes
- Reference to the original complaint
- Assignment details including which admin created the order
- Target maintenance staff member for assignment
- Engineering approval information and notes
- Estimated completion date for planning purposes
- Assignment notes with specific instructions

MAINTENANCE REPORTING
Upon completion of assigned work, maintenance staff submit comprehensive reports that include:
- Detailed description of the work actually performed
- Complete list of materials used with quantities
- Total hours worked on the project
- Additional notes about the completion process
- Photos documenting the completed work
- Assessment of work quality completed
- Indication of whether follow-up work is required

STEP-BY-STEP USER WORKFLOWS

CONSUMER WORKFLOW - SUBMITTING A COMPLAINT
Step 1: Log into the system using your assigned username and password
Step 2: Navigate to the New Complaint section
Step 3: Enter a clear title that summarizes your water issue
Step 4: Provide detailed description including when the problem started, how severe it is, and any relevant details
Step 5: Specify the exact location of the problem with street address or landmark references
Step 6: Select appropriate priority level based on the severity of the issue
Step 7: Submit the complaint and note the complaint number provided for future reference
Step 8: Monitor your complaint status through the My Complaints section

ADMIN WORKFLOW - PROCESSING COMPLAINTS
Step 1: Review new complaints in the Pending Complaints queue
Step 2: Verify complaint information and add administrative notes if needed
Step 3: Update complaint status to Reviewed once initial assessment is complete
Step 4: Forward appropriate complaints to Engineering by changing status to Submitted to Engineering
Step 5: Monitor engineering assessments and approvals
Step 6: For approved complaints, create new work orders with detailed assignment information
Step 7: Assign work orders to appropriate maintenance staff based on location and expertise
Step 8: Track work order progress and verify completion when maintenance reports are submitted

ENGINEERING WORKFLOW - TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT
Step 1: Review complaints in the Engineering Review queue
Step 2: Conduct site visits or technical analysis as required for proper assessment
Step 3: Create approval or decline decision based on technical findings
Step 4: Document detailed engineering assessment with technical specifications
Step 5: For approved items, provide comprehensive material recommendations and hour estimates
Step 6: Add any special instructions, safety requirements, or technical notes
Step 7: Submit assessment to return complaint to admin for work order creation

MAINTENANCE WORKFLOW - COMPLETING WORK
Step 1: Check assigned work orders in your personal work queue
Step 2: Review work order details, engineering notes, and material requirements
Step 3: Update work order status to In Progress when beginning work
Step 4: Perform required maintenance work according to engineering specifications
Step 5: Document all work performed with detailed descriptions
Step 6: Record exact materials used including quantities and part numbers
Step 7: Track total hours worked including start and end times
Step 8: Take photos documenting completed work and any relevant before/after conditions
Step 9: Submit comprehensive maintenance report upon work completion
Step 10: Update work order status to Completed

SYSTEM NAVIGATION AND INTERFACE

DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
Upon logging in, users see a dashboard customized to their role with quick access to relevant functions and current work items. The dashboard displays summary statistics and recent activity relevant to the user's responsibilities.

MENU SYSTEM
The main navigation menu provides access to all system functions organized by user role. Common menu items include Dashboard, Complaints, Work Orders, Reports, and User Profile settings.

SEARCH AND FILTERING
Users can search for specific complaints, work orders, or other records using various criteria including date ranges, status, location, assigned personnel, and keyword searches.

NOTIFICATIONS
The system provides automated notifications for status changes, assignment updates, and other relevant events. Users can configure notification preferences in their profile settings.

TROUBLESHOOTING AND SUPPORT

COMMON ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS
If you cannot log into the system, verify your username and password are entered correctly and contact your system administrator for account assistance.

If complaint status is not updating, refresh your browser page and verify you are viewing the most current information.

If work order assignments are not appearing, check your user role permissions and confirm you are logged in with the correct account.

For technical issues with submitting forms or uploading photos, try using a different web browser or clearing your browser cache and cookies.

PASSWORD AND ACCOUNT ISSUES
Password reset functionality is available through the login screen for users who forget their credentials. Contact your administrator if you need assistance with account access or permission changes.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
The system is web-based and requires a modern internet browser with JavaScript enabled. For optimal performance, use the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge browsers.

Photo upload functionality requires a browser that supports file uploads and may have file size limitations for optimal performance.

SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION
All user data is encrypted and protected according to industry standards. User passwords are securely hashed and stored safely. Report any suspected security issues to your system administrator immediately.

Users should log out completely when finished using the system, especially on shared computers. Never share login credentials with other users.

DATA BACKUP AND RECOVERY
All system data is automatically backed up on a regular schedule. In the event of data loss or system issues, contact your administrator for assistance with data recovery.

CONTACT INFORMATION AND SUPPORT
For technical support issues, contact your system administrator or IT support team with details about the problem including error messages, browser type, and steps that led to the issue.

For questions about complaint processing or workflow procedures, contact your department supervisor or training coordinator.

For urgent water system emergencies outside normal business hours, use established emergency contact procedures rather than the maintenance log system.

TRAINING AND ADDITIONAL RESOURCES
New users should complete role-specific training before using the system independently. Training materials and user guides are available through your supervisor or training department.

Regular refresher training sessions may be scheduled to cover system updates or new features as they become available.

Advanced users may have access to additional features or reporting capabilities depending on their role and responsibilities within the organization.
